Almost no false alarms

Out of 100 legitimate messages, at most one was wrongly flagged. People keep trusting an app that rarely cries wolf.

It misses scams without clues

Scams with no link or obvious keyword slip through: fake job offers, "officer" calls, prize emails, fake tech support, crypto schemes.

Links and apps are checked separately

This page covers message wording only. Links and apps also go to VirusTotal and Google Safe Browsing, and APKs to Androguard and YARA.

Method. A message counts as a threat if the engine says "scam" or "suspicious". Counting only "scam" the recall falls to 6 of 100 (server) and 4 of 100 (on-device), because most scams are labelled "suspicious".
Data. dataset/ in the repo: 200 messages written by the team from documented Indian scam patterns. It is small and self-made, so treat these as a baseline, not a guarantee. No independent test set yet.
