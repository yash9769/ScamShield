# ScamShield — User Manual

## Getting Started

Open ScamShield on your Android or iOS device. The app opens to the **Home** screen showing your shield status and scan statistics.

---

## Navigation

The bottom navigation bar has 5 tabs:

| Tab | Icon | Purpose |
|-----|------|---------|
| **HOME** | 🏠 | Dashboard, stats, recent threats |
| **SCAN** | 🔍 | Analyse a message or link |
| **HISTORY** | 🕐 | View all past scans |
| **LEARN** | 📚 | Education module & quiz |
| **PROFILE** | 👤 | Settings, privacy, data |

---

## How to Scan a Message

1. Tap the **SCAN** tab.
2. Paste or type the suspicious message into the text area.
3. Tap **ANALYSE NOW**.
4. View the result:
   - **🟢 SAFE** — No significant threats detected.
   - **🟡 SUSPICIOUS** — Warning signals found. Do not share personal data.
   - **🔴 SCAM** — High-confidence scam. Do not click links or call numbers.
5. Review the detailed reason cards below the result.
6. The scan is automatically saved to your History.

---

## Scan History

1. Tap the **HISTORY** tab.
2. Use the **filter chips** to view All / Threats / Suspicious / Safe.
3. Tap the **search icon** to search by message content.
4. **Swipe left** on any record to delete it.
5. Tap the **trash icon** in the top-right to clear all history.
6. The summary stats card shows total scans, threats, safe count, and average risk.

## Education Module

### Daily Tip
A new security tip appears every day on the Learn screen.

### Scam Encyclopedia
1. Tap any article card to read the full article.
2. Tap **Mark as Read & Earn Badge** to unlock the badge and earn points.

### Quiz
1. Tap **START QUIZ** to begin a 15-question quiz.
2. Select your answer for each question.
3. Read the explanation after each answer.
4. See your result and score at the end.
5. Passing (≥70%) unlocks the **Quiz Master 🏆** badge.

### Badges
Badges are awarded for:
- Reading each article
- Passing a quiz
- Maintaining a 7-day streak

### Vigilance Score
Your score (0–100) is calculated from points earned, streak, and articles read.

---

## Privacy Settings

Go to **PROFILE** → scroll to **Privacy & Data**:

| Setting | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Data Consent | Toggle to consent to local data storage |
| Auto-Delete History | Automatically delete scans after 7/30/90 days |
| Privacy Policy | View the full privacy policy |
| Delete All Data | Permanently erase all local data |

---

## Offline Mode

If your device loses internet connectivity, a yellow banner appears at the top of every screen. The local scam detection engine continues to work fully offline. Only the AI-powered analysis (requires internet) will be unavailable.

---

## Tips for Best Results

- Paste the **full** message text including any URLs.
- For voice call scams, type out key phrases said by the caller.
- Even if the result shows SAFE, never share OTPs, PINs, or passwords.
- Use the Education module to learn to spot scams yourself.
